# Domain vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Synthetic case | An invented scenario with no real person or contact details. |
| Broker fixture | An invented example organization with a reserved `.example` domain. |
| Plan item | A proposed manual step for one synthetic case and broker fixture. |
| Evidence entry | An append-only account of a simulated action, its date, source, and observation. |
| Evidence artifact | A synthetic note or fixture document associated with an entry. |
| Status | A derived view of recorded events; it cannot prove a real removal. |
| Follow-up date | A displayed planning date that does not schedule a notification or job. |
